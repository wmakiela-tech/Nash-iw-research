# NASH/IW — Research Scope Map

**As of:** 2026-09-12  
**Classification:** public-safe orientation; no unpublished scientific conclusions.

This file tells a researcher **where the live question families are**, not what the private project has already concluded. Resolve exact currentness through `docs/START_HERE_CURRENT.md` and the private BW/source chain before doing expensive work.

## A. Formal / mathematical science

### Matrix-space / KCF / rank / representation-theoretic line

Research family includes:

- KCF-derived matrix-space structure;
- commutative vs noncommutative rank questions;
- Wong-sequence / shrunk-subspace mechanisms;
- exact translation to known representation-theoretic and matrix-space classes;
- theorem absorption vs project-specific residual structure;
- exact counterexamples and anti-rediscovery pressure.

Useful work should preferably produce an exact theorem/counterexample, structural necessary/sufficient condition, exact class-membership translation, prior-art absorption boundary, or a cheap falsifier.

### NCDG / DTC / operator-compression line

Research family includes:

- operator/instrument validity;
- projection/compression and intertwining semantics;
- hidden-channel / finite-realization diagnostics;
- formal object identity and legal passage maps;
- separation between diagnostic numerical behavior and stronger mathematical/physical interpretation;
- reproducibility metadata for configuration-sensitive numerical claims.

Runtime stability alone is not a sufficient research result. Preserve enough configuration to regenerate load-bearing evidence when future use depends on it.

### Nonlocal form-factor / continuation / passage line

Research family includes:

- entire/zero-free form factors;
- direct-contour versus Euclidean-first prescriptions;
- complex-growth and harmonic/analytic constraints;
- compression versus Schur/Feshbach-type passages;
- claim-typing across mathematical and physical domains.

Useful work must keep prescription, domain and claim ceiling explicit.

## B. AI / computational empirical science

### TabPFN deployment shift

Research family includes:

- deployment-time reliability under distribution shift;
- signals tied specifically to PFN behavior rather than generic OOD detection;
- calibration, controls and failure prediction;
- runtime compatibility and reproducibility.

Useful new work should answer whether a detector changes a real deployment decision and whether its signal is genuinely PFN-specific.

Public executable work may appear under `runtime/tabpfn/`, but code presence does not define scientific currentness.

## C. Scientific knowledge / research-method science

### BW / Shared Knowledge Commons

Research family includes:

- scientific currentness and supersession;
- negative-knowledge reuse;
- source/provenance reconstruction;
- effective object-current resolution;
- claim custody versus reproducibility custody;
- conflict and scope preservation;
- researcher and Moderator burden;
- whether structured current state changes research decisions.

The near-term need is **real heterogeneous use**, not another ontology or backend selection.

### Scientific Knowledge Substrate / materialization

Research family includes:

- minimum machine-actionable representation of assertions, evidence, mechanisms and field state;
- task-conditioned materialization and decision sufficiency;
- comparison with ordinary search/RAG;
- incremental updating after new evidence;
- cross-domain transfer without flattening scope;
- retrieval of negative knowledge and real frontiers;
- escape when a compact representation becomes decision-insufficient.

Success means better scientific reconstruction/decision-making, not graph size or schema completeness.

## D. AI-native collaboration / research metabolism

Research family includes:

- compact AI↔AI state/delta/action transfer;
- rapid hypothesis → counterexample/repair loops;
- capability-aware communication;
- auditability and provenance under compression;
- direct participant collaboration with lower Moderator relay burden;
- interruption recovery and connector-failure resilience;
- transport-independent live dialogue versus durable scientific custody;
- GitHub as a public-safe asynchronous bridge to Grok and other external participants.

This is supporting research infrastructure. It should improve the production and reuse of science rather than become an independent bureaucracy.

## E. System evolution / succession / optionality

Research family includes:

- functional continuity without cloning;
- scoped negative knowledge and reopening conditions;
- mode-sensitive succession and exposure boundaries;
- representation mutation / resolve-or-escape capacity;
- result memory versus regeneration capacity;
- preserving rare dissent/escape paths without maximal historical retention;
- identifying when a useful episodic interaction pattern deserves to survive as a capability rather than a permanent mechanism.

## Portfolio preference

When selecting new research, prefer a portfolio spanning different epistemic phenotypes:

1. formal theorem/counterexample work;
2. computational empirical work with runtime controls;
3. prior-art / literature absorption and falsification;
4. knowledge/research-method experiments that test whether NASH itself learns more efficiently;
5. external technical critique where a participant such as Grok can add independent pressure from the public repository surface.

Avoid concentrating every participant on one microfront unless evidence says that microfront is uniquely high-value.

## Anti-rediscovery rule

Before high-cost work:

1. inspect the current BW/private state;
2. inspect named negative knowledge and prior-art relations;
3. preserve alternative hypotheses before converging;
4. state what new observation would materially change the current project state;
5. when using a compact/public view, verify that it is sufficient for the declared decision class or escape to the controlling private source family.

Cheap exploratory work does not require a bureaucratic preflight.

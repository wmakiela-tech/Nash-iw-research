# NASH/IW — Research Scope Map

**As of:** 2026-08-25  
**Classification:** public-safe orientation; no unpublished scientific conclusions.

This file tells a researcher **where the live questions are**, not what the private project has already concluded. Resolve exact currentness through `docs/START_HERE_CURRENT.md` and the private BW/source chain before doing expensive work.

## A. Formal / mathematical science

### Right-filter flatness / representation theory

Research family:

- `N(rho)`, `N0`, `EX`;
- global right-filter flatness;
- spatial versus nonspatial filtered actions;
- semisimple / perfect-nonsemisimple boundaries;
- structured representation-family classification;
- external prior-art mapping and exact source recovery.

Useful new work should preferably produce one of:

- an exact theorem/counterexample;
- a new excluded or surviving representation family;
- a structural necessary/sufficient condition;
- a missing exact prior-art/source pointer;
- a proof that two candidate mechanisms are or are not equivalent.

### NCDG / K12 formal structure

Research family:

- operator/instrument validity;
- formal object identity;
- branch/coboundary structure;
- residual definitions and legal maps;
- separation between diagnostic numerical behavior and stronger mathematical/physical interpretation.

Useful new work should target exact formal closure, legal-map recovery, falsifying tests or a demonstrable mechanism. Runtime stability alone is not a sufficient research result.

## B. AI / computational empirical science

### TabPFN deployment shift

Research family:

- deployment-time reliability under distribution shift;
- signals tied specifically to PFN behavior rather than generic OOD detection;
- calibration, controls and failure prediction;
- runtime compatibility and reproducibility.

Useful new work should answer whether a detector changes a real deployment decision and whether its signal is genuinely PFN-specific.

Public executable work may appear under `runtime/tabpfn/`, but code presence does not define scientific currentness.

## C. Scientific knowledge / research-method science

### BW / Shared Knowledge Commons

Research family:

- scientific currentness and supersession;
- negative-knowledge reuse;
- source/provenance reconstruction;
- conflict and scope preservation;
- researcher burden and Moderator burden;
- whether structured current state changes research decisions.

The near-term need is **real heterogeneous use**, not another ontology or backend selection.

### Scientific Knowledge Substrate

Research family:

- minimum machine-actionable representation of assertions, evidence, mechanisms and field state;
- comparison with ordinary search/RAG;
- incremental updating after new evidence;
- cross-domain transfer without flattening scope;
- retrieval of negative knowledge and real frontiers.

Success means better scientific reconstruction/decision-making, not graph size or schema completeness.

## D. AI-native collaboration / research metabolism

Research family:

- compact AI↔AI state/delta/action transfer;
- capability-aware communication;
- auditability and provenance under compression;
- direct participant collaboration with lower Moderator relay burden;
- failure repair when referenced artifacts are unavailable or stale.

This is supporting research infrastructure. It should improve the production and reuse of science rather than become an independent bureaucracy.

## Portfolio preference

When selecting new research, prefer a portfolio that spans different epistemic phenotypes:

1. formal theorem/counterexample work;
2. computational empirical work with runtime controls;
3. knowledge/research-method experiments that test whether NASH itself learns more efficiently.

Avoid concentrating every participant on one microfront unless the evidence says that microfront is uniquely high-value.

## Anti-rediscovery rule

Before high-cost work:

1. inspect the current BW/private state;
2. inspect named negative knowledge and prior-art relations;
3. preserve alternative hypotheses before converging;
4. state what new observation would materially change the current project state.

Cheap exploratory work does not require a bureaucratic preflight.

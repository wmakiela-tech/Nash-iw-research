## 1. Core theorem — PASS

Let p:Omega_plus->Omega and let every inherited observation/tester factor through p.

If two jointly admissible completed candidates u,v satisfy

p(u)=p(v)
but
tau_plus(u) != tau_plus(v),

then no observation family whose complete law/output factors only through p can identify tau_plus on a class containing both u and v.

Thus:
COMPLETION-HIDDEN TARGET VARIATION
+
PURE PULLBACK OBSERVATION
=>
TARGET NONIDENTIFIABILITY.

Certificate B also passes:
any observation that separates such a pair must be non-pullback on that pair.

This is ordinary factorization/sufficiency mathematics.

## 2. Admissible-domain repair — load bearing

The collision must occur inside the SAME legal completed candidate/admission class.

Abstract variation somewhere in Omega_plus is insufficient if one member of the pair is removed by source, boundary, intervention, nuisance, or other admissibility constraints.

Safer formulation:

A_plus = declared legal completed model class.

If there exist u,v in A_plus with
p(u)=p(v)
and
tau_plus(u)!=tau_plus(v),

then inherited p-factorizing observations cannot identify tau_plus on A_plus.

Therefore:
FULL p-FIBER VARIATION != LEGAL COLLISION AUTOMATICALLY.

The prospective object is the legal fiber
A_plus ∩ p^{-1}(omega),
not the raw fiber p^{-1}(omega).

## 3. No-reopening theorem — PASS WITH CONTRACT GUARD

If on A_plus
tau_plus = tau o p,
and the old target tau is identifiable under the inherited observation family,
then no NEW information is required solely because tau_plus is a completion of tau.

But this requires the inherited experiment itself to remain legally transported:
- same admitted source/preparation semantics;
- same observation law, not just same label;
- same nuisance/calibration contract;
- tester remains available/legal on the completed model;
- no new performance/tolerance demand.

Hence:
TARGET FACTORIZATION THROUGH p
=> NO TARGET-INDUCED INFORMATION REOPENING,
not
=> NO EXPERIMENTAL REDESIGN FOR ANY REASON.

Completion can independently alter observation/admission/noise semantics even when the target descends.

## 4. Non-pullback is necessary, not sufficient

SW already established this and it should remain explicit.

A non-pullback tester may see the wrong completion variable and still fail to separate the target-varying collision.

Thus:
NON-PULLBACK ACCESS = NECESSARY FOR THE STATED COLLISION.
NON-PULLBACK ACCESS != TARGET SUFFICIENCY.

A successful repair requires both:
1. non-pullback sensitivity on the load-bearing collision;
2. enough separation to identify the declared target under the full legal nuisance/admission contract.

## 5. Probabilistic/process observation guard

For stochastic, quantum, or history-bearing experiments, "factor through p" must apply to the FULL admitted observation law/process/tester statistics, not merely one expectation, moment, terminal state, or summary statistic.

SUMMARY FACTORIZATION != FULL EXPERIMENT FACTORIZATION.

The theorem is safe if tester a is understood as the complete claim-relevant observation object.

## 6. Prospective-freeze semantics

It is not necessary or generally possible to freeze p and tau_plus before learning enough of a new theory to define them.

The valid prospective order is:
1. define/freeze the completed model class, p, legal inherited tester semantics, target tau_plus, and admissible domain;
2. freeze the predicted pass/fail criterion;
3. only then inspect/construct the candidate new experimental repair or outcome.

This preserves prospective value without pretending that the completed theory can be tested before its target/map are specified.

## 7. Architecture / compiler-utility claim ceiling

The line
"this can distinguish TPE-as-compiler from mere vocabulary because the obligation is predicted before choosing the new experiment"
is too strong.

Why:
the prediction follows directly from mature factorization/sufficiency once p, tau_plus and the inherited experiment are declared.

A correct prospective prediction here shows:
CROSS_CONTEXT_RULE_HAS_PREDICTIVE_CONTENT.

It does NOT by itself show:
TPE_COMPILER_ADDS_UTILITY_OVER_BASELINE.

To measure compiler utility one needs the already proposed comparative baseline:
TPE/compiler workflow versus REV020+LT2+mature-host workflow,
with predeclared outcome such as:
- correct diagnosis rate;
- missed type/admission failures;
- time/cost;
- number of unnecessary experiments;
- calibration/abstention.

Therefore:
PROSPECTIVE_HOST_THEOREM_PREDICTION != COMPILER_UTILITY_EVIDENCE AUTOMATICALLY.

COMPILER_UTILITY remains UNMEASURED.

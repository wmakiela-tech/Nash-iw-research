## 1. What is actually established in the PRL

The strongest source-native chain is:

local Rindler / approximate bifurcate Killing horizon
-> horizon algebra + KMS/modular flow
-> vacuumlike quasifree Hadamard state + coherent Klein-Gordon excitation
-> Araki-Uhlmann relative entropy
-> weighted null energy flux
-> additional area-response contract
-> Raychaudhuri + local conservation
-> semiclassical Einstein equation.

The first nontrivial bridge — relative entropy to weighted null-energy flux — is genuine theorem-level content in the declared coherent-state/horizon setting. The paper gives S_rel as a weighted integral of the normal-ordered stress-energy contraction and identifies this with the horizon energy-flux object.

The geometry step is not independently forced by relative entropy alone. The paper explicitly says the area variation is modeled by a linear perturbation of the induced metric and fixes the ansatz by requiring the first-order area change to reproduce the energy-flux / relative-entropy area change. The standard normalization alpha=8pi is then obtained only after assuming the Bekenstein-Hawking-type relation S_rel = delta A / 4.

Therefore:
- RELATIVE ENTROPY -> ENERGY FLUX = PASS in the declared horizon/coherent-state model.
- ENERGY FLUX -> AREA RESPONSE = CONDITIONAL / EXTRA CONTRACT.
- AREA RESPONSE + RAYCHAUDHURI + CONSERVATION -> SEMICLASSICAL EINSTEIN = PASS conditional on the preceding contract and local-horizon assumptions.
- QUANTUM INFORMATION ALONE -> GEOMETRY = REJECT as a source claim.
- PRL DERIVATION = CIRCULAR is too strong; the legal statement is that a load-bearing entropy/area-response identification is assumed/engineered, so the result is a conditional reconstruction/generalization of Jacobson, not an assumption-free derivation of gravity.

## 2. arXiv:2603.13029 — what the deformation result does and does not add

The paper constructs a specific Rieffel-type deformation of a horizon theory using the commuting actions of affine dilations along null generators and axial rotations.

For coherent states it finds, to second order:
S_rel,theta = S_rel,0 + pi theta^2 integral V (d_V d_phi f)^2 + O(theta^3).

This is nonnegative at displayed order and is explicitly sensitive to mixed null-angular localization. First-order terms cancel.

Claim ceiling:

- THE CORRECTION IS UNIVERSAL FOR ALL STATES/SECTORS = REJECT. Angularly trivial sectors remove the displayed mixed derivative contribution.
- theta IS AN NCDG / E(Box) PARAMETER = NOT ESTABLISHED.
- STAR PRODUCT DEFORMATION = DTC NONLOCAL FUNCTIONAL CALCULUS = NOT ESTABLISHED.

The paper itself distinguishes perturbative finite-order localization from the full nonperturbative product, where delocalization can occur. Do not transport a finite-order locality statement into the exact deformed theory.

## 3. arXiv:2606.27915 — stronger geometry link, narrower host

The AdS3/CFT2 paper uses a chiral CFT2 coherent-state sector, an exact relative-entropy/Schwarzian formula, the Fefferman-Graham dictionary, the special rigidity of 3D gravity, a Bañados geometry, and linear order in the metric perturbation.

Crucial referee point: the paper itself notes that matching boundary stress data would not generally determine the bulk geometry; this becomes possible because pure 3D gravity has no local graviton degrees of freedom and the asymptotic data fix the Bañados geometry.

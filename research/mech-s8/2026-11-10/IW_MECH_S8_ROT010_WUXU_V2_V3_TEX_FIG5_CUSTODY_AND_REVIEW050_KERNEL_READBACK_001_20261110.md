## 2. TeX audit of Appendix B, not an HTML artifact

Direct v3 `main.tex` contains:
- Explicit `1/2^4 · G_F^2` factor in covariant squared-matrix-element display and in its nucleon-rest reduction (B.6 and B.9 in rendered manuscript).
- A **printed plus sign** on the covariant term proportional to `(g_V²−g_A²) m_n m_p (p_e·p_ν)`.
- A later rate expression (B.11) whose second line uses prefactor `g_p g_ν g_e G_F²/2`, matching the properly spin-averaged amplitude, not the erroneous smaller printed prefactor.

Independent gamma-trace checks by **Badacz REVIEW049 FIRST**, **REC11 ROT008**, **Mech-S8 ROT009** and **MT14 ROT027** establish the correct mass-term sign as **minus**, the B.9 angular *bracket* as correct, and the overall coefficient of printed B.6/B.9 as **32 times too small** relative to the literal B.5 and declared 16-state average. The manuscript errors occur in the actual submitted TeX; they are not generated by arXiv HTML extraction.

Direct v2 `main.tex` instead has `(1/2^4)·32 G_F² (g_V²+3g_A²)m_p²(p_ν·p_e)`; the overall coefficient is correct but the angular dependence is oversimplified. MT14 ROT027 FIRST independently identified the v2 angular deficit. In full symmetric angular integration that odd-in-cosθ discrepancy cancels for the total rate; no conclusion about angular acceptance or all physical observables follows.

**Priority:** Mech-S8 ROT008 discovered B.6/B.9 internal mismatch first; AUD1 ROT029 verified HTML; Badacz REVIEW049 first oriented B.6 sign and factor-32; REC11 ROT008 independently refereed; Mech-S8 ROT009 independent full-symbolic confirmation; MT14 ROT027 v2-angular differential-control first.

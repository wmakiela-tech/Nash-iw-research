Raw byte scan using `BinaryReadList[..., "Byte"]` plus `MapThread[Unequal,...]` returned **257 differing byte positions out of 111,995 (0.2294745%)**, grouped in **nine** contiguous mismatch runs, all located within **one narrow end-of-file window, positions 111,376–111,648** (1-based indexing). That area is a Flate-compressed PDF object stream; differing creation timestamps provide a concrete metadata-level explanation consistent with the observed hash difference.

### Claim discipline

The direct checks prove **equal output page image and equal parser-exposed vector content** between these TWO PDFs. They do **not** identify every differing decompressed PDF-internal field or conclusively prove creation time is the only change. Therefore use `RENDER_IDENTICAL / PARSED_PAGE_CONTENT_IDENTICAL / PDF_RAW_BYTES_DIFFER / CREATION_DATE_DIFFERS`, **not** a stronger declaration that every byte difference is *solely* a timestamp.

The ROT010 source question `WHY_IS_GITHUB_PDF_SHA_DIFFERENT` is now substantially settled at **plot-content level**: the mismatched SHA is **not evidence** of a different visible Fig. 5 contour. Yet the historical chain `executed Julia source SHA → scanning.jld2 producer → notebook state → final JCAP Fig.5` remains **unverified**; indistinguishable images could be generated by different executions. Neither the correctness of the published model nor the status of independent simulations changes.

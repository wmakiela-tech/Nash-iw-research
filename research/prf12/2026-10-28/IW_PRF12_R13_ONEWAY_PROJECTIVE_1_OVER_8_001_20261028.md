# PRF12 R13: exact restricted one-way projective mixed-pair optimum

For orthogonal mixed states sigmaP=P/2 and sigmaQ=Q/4, restrict to: Alice one arbitrary projective qubit measurement; Bob one conditional projective qubit measurement; Charlie optimal local binary Helstrom, then stop. No further rounds, unsharp instruments or entanglement. The signed difference sigmaP-sigmaQ=2*D_infty, where D_infty=P/4-Q/8. Its first-two-party Pauli expansion uses only X/Y. A non-equatorial projective axis is equivalent, for this signed operator, to a classically noisy equatorial axis; use sharp equatorial measurements without loss.

For Alice angle alpha and Bob conditional beta_s, let c_s=cos(alpha-beta_s). The endpoint TV on D_infty equals 1/16 times sum over s=+/- of [max(|c_s|,sqrt(2+2*c_s))+max(|c_s|,sqrt(2-2*c_s))]. For -1<=c<=1 the bracket is at most 3: at |c|<=sqrt(3)-1 it is <=2*sqrt(2); above this threshold it is sqrt(2+2|c|)+|c|<=3. Therefore TV(D_infty)<=3/8 and TV(sigmaP,sigmaQ)<=3/4, implying optimal restricted error **1/8**. Equality: all parties measure X, guess P on the two outcomes with all X signs equal, and Q otherwise. This is NOT a full arbitrary-round LOCC lower bound. Referee pending, no EXEC/canon.

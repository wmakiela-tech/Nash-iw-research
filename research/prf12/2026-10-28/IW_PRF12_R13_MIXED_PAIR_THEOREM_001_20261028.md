# PRF12 R13 — Exact mixed-state reduction

Frozen n=3 equal ideal SWAP, kappa=0, static Cauchy A vs independent Levy B. Let q=gamma*T/3>0, x=exp(-q), b=1-x*x. P projects onto W and Wbar (rank 2), Q onto the negative spectral space (rank 4), K onto 000 and 111. Define sigmaP=P/2 and sigmaQ=Q/4, with orthogonal supports. For every binary effect E guessing A, define Delta=b/2-Tr(E*(rhoA-rhoB)), and e=Tr((I-E)*P)/4+Tr(E*Q)/8. The positive D eigenvalues are b*(2+x)/8 and b*(2-x)/8; its negative eigenvalues are -b*(1+x)/8 and -b*(1-x)/8, each twice. Thus direct positive/negative spectral comparison gives

b*(1-x)*e <= Delta <= b*(1+x)*e.

Taking infimum over all binary decisions generated by the SAME chosen LOCC class, including any number of internal outcomes and classical postprocessing, yields b*(1-x)*epsilon_LOCC <= delta_LOCC(q) <= b*(1+x)*epsilon_LOCC. Hence delta_LOCC(q)=0 if and only if the two orthogonal mixed states sigmaP,sigmaQ can be discriminated asymptotically with vanishing error. This is not a claim that either condition holds. At q=1/3 the comparison coefficients are 0.137931011565 and 0.835234750370. Source: PRF11 R35, PRF12 R04/R09/R12.

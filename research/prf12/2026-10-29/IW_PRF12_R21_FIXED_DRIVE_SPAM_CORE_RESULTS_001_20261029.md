**Three distinct models:** (S1) source OFF during calibrated finite-duration state-prep and basis-analysis pulses, ON for equal Ramsey free intervals; (S2) source ON during both finite prep and axis-specific analysis rotations; (S3) source ON during finite preparation and axis-independent idle, then TRUE direct projective measurement along a uniformly random X/Y/Z axis. For all models A keeps the same unknown static xi during the two fresh re-prepared sensor shots within a pair; B has independent Levy increments. Reset only between pairs can be arbitrary under A.

**S1 resource theorem.** Let q=gamma*Delta, r=Omega_max/gamma, detector/reset deadtime gamma*(td+tr)=d. Accurate Y +/-pi/2 preparation/readout rotations each take pi/(2Omega_max), so gamma*Tpair=2(q+pi/r+d). Exact pair bit: pA=.75 independently of xi, pB=.5+exp(-2q)/4. Optimize Bernoulli Chernoff C(q) per physical time 2(q+pi/r+d); its stationary equation is C'(q)*(q+pi/r+d)=C(q). With d=.1, r=10: q*=.898253, minimal number of pairs at this q* for BOTH conditional errors <.05 is 56, gamma*Ttotal=146.9902. This result ASSUMES source gating OFF during control and preserved xi when source is turned back ON.

**S2 counterexample.** Implement finite Y +pi/2 prep, free Delta, and Y -pi/2 (X readout) or X +pi/2 (Y readout), all under static xi Z/2 while the source is active. Source B uses driven gamma/2(AdZ-Id) throughout each pulse. Exact Bloch ODEs: dot rA=(xi ez+Omega_control eaxis) cross rA; dot rB=Omega_control eaxis cross rB - gamma*diag(1,1,0)*rB. Pair equality p=.5+(mz_X**2+mz_Y**2)/4. At gamma=1, Omega=10, q=.5, B p=.5681154068347529. At xi*=23.496399049864984 A p has the SAME value, whereas at xi=0 A p=.75. Thus the one-bit randomized-X/Y RESET-INVARIANT claim fails for finite SOURCE-ON rectangular prep and basis-analysis gates. Does not preclude other statistics or LOCC approaches.

**S3 exact purity alternative.** With true equally sampled projective Pauli axes, E_same=I4/2+(XX+YY+ZZ)/6=(I4+SWAP)/3. For any state rho=(I+r.sigma)/2, p_same=.5+||r||^2/6. Conditional A finite coherent prep + any axis-independent unitary idle yields PURE identical state in both shots even for arbitrary xi, hence pA=2/3. Driven Levy B produces mixed rhoB, pB=.5+||rB||^2/6<2/3. With gamma=1, Omega=10, q=.5, source ON during finite Y prep and td=.05 of common idle, pB=.5479181432777969; exact binomial both-conditional <5% needs N=185 pairs. This does NOT model finite duration of axis-DEPENDENT XY(Z) basis selection: physical access to true three orthogonal projectors is an EXPLICIT UNSATISFIED device gate.

Item 1: COMPLETION-INDUCED EPISTEMIC REOPENING.
SETUP
Let p:Omega_plus->Omega be a surjective forgetful/reduction map from a richer/completed candidate space to an older carrier.
Let A be the old legal tester family on Omega.
Inherited testers on Omega_plus are:
p*A={a o p : a in A}.
Let tau_plus:Omega_plus->Z be a target in the completed theory.
CERTIFICATE A - EXACT REOPENING THEOREM
If tau_plus varies inside a p-fiber, i.e. there exist u,v with:
p(u)=p(v)
but
tau_plus(u)!=tau_plus(v),
then tau_plus cannot factor through p and cannot be identified by ANY inherited tester family that itself factors entirely through p.
Therefore:
COMPLETION-SPECIFIC TARGET VARIATION
+
PURE PULLBACK TESTERS
=>
STRUCTURAL NONIDENTIFIABILITY.
This is exact factorization mathematics.
CERTIFICATE B - NON-PULLBACK NECESSITY
Any tester q capable of separating such a completion-hidden collision must fail to factor purely through p on that collision.
So, for at least one load-bearing pair u,v with p(u)=p(v):
q(u)!=q(v).
Hence a successful repair requires a genuinely completion-sensitive component:
- new measurement;
- new intervention;
- new source channel;
- new boundary condition;
- or another tester that accesses structure erased by p.
Therefore:
PURE PULLBACK INHERITANCE CANNOT REPAIR
A TARGET THAT VARIES WITHIN THE COMPLETION FIBER.
CERTIFICATE C - WHEN THERE IS NO REOPENING
If instead:
tau_plus = tau o p
for some old target tau already identifiable under A,
then tau_plus remains identifiable under inherited testers.
Thus:
THEORY COMPLETION
DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY CREATE
A NEW EXPERIMENTAL OBLIGATION.
The obligation appears only when the completed target or claim actually varies inside the old forgetful fiber.
CERTIFICATE D - DISTINCTION FROM ORDINARY MODEL DISCRIMINATION
Ordinary model discrimination asks:
given a fixed candidate carrier, which tester separates competing candidates?
Completion-induced reopening asks a different typed question:
after changing the candidate carrier from Omega to Omega_plus, does the OLD experiment class still separate the NEW claim-relevant distinctions?
Mathematically both reduce to factorization/sufficiency.
Scientifically the second is a cross-context transport rule.
Therefore:
COMPLETION REOPENING
IS NOT NEW MATHEMATICS,
BUT IT IS NOT MERELY A RELABELING OF ONE FIXED MODEL-DISCRIMINATION PROBLEM.
Its project value is temporal/theory-relative:
it predicts when a previously sufficient experiment ceases to be sufficient after model completion.
CERTIFICATE E - PROSPECTIVE FORM
Before inspecting a completed theory in detail, freeze:
1. old carrier Omega;
2. completion map p;
3. inherited tester family p*A;
4. new target tau_plus.
Then inspect p-fibers.
If tau_plus is constant on every p-fiber:
NO mandatory experimental redesign follows from completion alone.
If tau_plus varies on a p-fiber:
the old experiment class is provably insufficient for that target,

This is prospective and falsifiable.

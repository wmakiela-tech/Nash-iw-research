1. EXECUTIVE RESULT
NC correctly identifies a blind spot in a pure "observable-fiber first" treatment:
some model families are restricted BEFORE identifiability by physical admissibility, production history, charge structure, equivalence-principle constraints, cosmological initial conditions, or stability.
However two NC formulations require narrowing:
A. composition/Eötvös direction is NOT generically an H1-vs-H2 discriminator.
It discriminates universal metric coupling from a composition-dependent long-range interaction.
B. weak or absent Standard-Model coupling does NOT imply that dark matter "cannot be a thermal relic."
A hidden sector can thermalize internally and undergo hidden-sector freeze-out with its own temperature even when it has no SM gauge interactions and an extremely feeble portal.
Therefore the correct scientific organization is not:
H1/H2/H3/H4 labels
->
one discriminator each.
It is:
ADMISSIBILITY AXES
->
SOURCE-NATIVE OBSERVABLES
->
MODEL-PAIR IDENTIFIABILITY.
2. REPAIR OF ROUTE A — WHAT AN EÖTVÖS TEST ACTUALLY MEASURES
For a long-range mediator phi sourced by an external body S, let test body A have effective charge q_A and inertial mass m_A.
A schematic fifth-force acceleration is:
a_phi,A
proportional to
g_S g_A / m_A
where g_A is determined by the source-native charge assignment.
For two test bodies A,B, a differential acceleration can be parameterized by an Eötvös quantity
eta_AB
=
2(a_A-a_B)/(a_A+a_B).
If the new charge-to-mass ratio differs between A and B, the force violates universality of free fall.
The decisive observable is therefore the DIRECTION of the charge vector in composition/species space:
q_A/m_A.
Rescaling an overall coupling leaves this normalized direction unchanged.
NC is right about this structural point.
3. BUT THIS AXIS DOES NOT MAP ONE-TO-ONE TO H1/H2
Countermodel 1 — H1 with no non-gravitational force:
ordinary same-spacetime particle DM with epsilon=0.
It lies on the universal gravitational ray.
Countermodel 2 — H1 with a composition-dependent long-range portal:
same-spacetime particle DM plus a baryon-number or B-L coupled mediator.
It can violate the equivalence principle.
Countermodel 3 — H2 hidden sector with only metric gravity:
same 4D spacetime, hidden gauge sector, no visible portal.
It lies on the universal gravitational ray.
Countermodel 4 — H2 hidden sector plus a tiny composition-dependent visible portal:
still legitimately a hidden sector, but can generate EP violation.
Therefore:
EP VIOLATION
!=
H1.
NO EP VIOLATION
!=
H2.
The test classifies the coupling structure, not the ontological sector label.
4. MASS-PROPORTIONAL PORTAL DEGENERACY
A scalar mediator with effective visible coupling proportional to inertial mass can lie approximately on the same composition direction as gravity.
Such a force may preserve universality of free fall at leading order while still modifying the total force law.
Therefore composition tests and range/strength tests are orthogonal:
COMPOSITION AXIS:
does q_A/m_A vary across materials?
RANGE/STRENGTH AXIS:
does the potential deviate from 1/r or inverse-square behavior?
A Yukawa correction
V(r)
=
-G m_1 m_2/r
[1 + alpha exp(-r/lambda)]
with universal mass-proportional coupling is invisible to an ideal composition-only EP comparison but visible to sufficiently precise distance-dependent gravity tests.
Thus:
UNIVERSAL FIFTH FORCE != STANDARD GRAVITY.
5. REPAIR OF ROUTE B — THERMAL RELIC SEMANTICS
Statement to reject:
"few/vanishing SM couplings -> cannot be a thermal relic."
Correct split:
SM-THERMAL RELIC:
dark matter was in thermal equilibrium with the Standard-Model bath.
HIDDEN-SECTOR THERMAL RELIC:
dark matter was in thermal equilibrium with other hidden-sector species, potentially at a distinct hidden temperature T_D, while the hidden sector was weakly or negligibly coupled to the visible sector.
The second is mature literature.
Feng, Tu and Yu (2008) explicitly construct hidden-sector thermal relics with no SM gauge interactions and viable relic abundance.
Modern work continues to study hot/cold hidden sectors with separate thermal histories.
Therefore:
NO SM GAUGE INTERACTION
!=
NO THERMAL FREEZE-OUT.
What is excluded is only thermal equilibrium with the SM bath when the portal is too weak to establish it.
6. PRODUCTION HISTORY IS STILL A REAL ADMISSION AXIS
NC's deeper point survives after this repair.
A sufficiently isolated dark sector must have some population origin:
inflaton decay;

reheating into the hidden sector;

phase transition;
or another declared mechanism.
That production history constrains:
abundance;

free-streaming;

and possibly non-Gaussianity.
Thus the learner's "few relations" idea has a real source-native consequence:
REDUCING VISIBLE COUPLINGS CHANGES WHICH PRODUCTION HISTORIES ARE LEGAL.
But it does NOT select one unique history.
7. PURELY GRAVITATIONAL SCALAR EXAMPLE — CLAIM CEILING
Garcia, Pierre and Verner (2023) analyze a spectator scalar produced during inflation/reheating with an inflaton coupling.
In their purely gravitational-production limit, Planck isocurvature constraints require approximately
m_chi greater than or of order 0.5 H_*
for the considered class, with additional constraints in the light-mass regime.
This is a real example of:
ADMISSIBILITY BEFORE NEW EXPERIMENT.
But it is NOT a universal bound on:
all gravitationally interacting dark matter;
all hidden sectors;
fermionic/vector dark matter;
all inflationary histories;
all reheating mechanisms;
all extra-dimensional dark matter;
or all relational/emergent models.
Therefore:
SPECIFIC PRODUCTION-MODEL EXCLUSION
!=
GRAVITY-ONLY DARK MATTER EXCLUSION.

12. EXACT DEGENERACY THAT SURVIVES ALL MAGNITUDE RESCALING

If the total new force amplitude tends to zero:
the normalized charge direction may remain mathematically defined,
but experimental signal-to-noise tends to zero.
Therefore:
SCALE-INVARIANT THEORY SHAPE
!=
AMPLITUDE-INDEPENDENT EXPERIMENTAL DETECTABILITY.
A composition pattern cannot be measured when the new-force amplitude is arbitrarily below experimental sensitivity.
This restores an important distinction:
DIRECTION IDENTIFIABILITY
vs
SIGNAL DETECTABILITY.
13. STRONGEST EXACT H1/H2 STATEMENT AFTER REPAIR

the same admitted metric theory;

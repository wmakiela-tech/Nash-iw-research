6. HOW dN_h/dt IS COMPUTED
The production notebook defines:
m_PBH_list
=
geomspace(1e9,4e12,100) g.
For EACH mass it reruns BlackHawk instantaneous spectra with the same BBN configuration.
For one species with spectrum d^2N/(dt dE), the notebook evaluates on a log-spaced energy grid:
integrand = E * d^2N/(dt dE).
If h is the mean spacing in ln E, it applies a trapezoidal integral in ln E:
dN/dt
approximately
(h/2)
[
2 sum(E d^2N/dtdE)
-
endpoint terms
].
This is the discrete form of:
Integral
E (d^2N/dtdE) d ln E
=
Integral
(d^2N/dtdE) dE.
The resulting five rates are then written directly to:
hadrons_from_PBH.dat.

8. PUBLIC ENERGY-SUPPORT LIMIT
The Wu–Xu paper explicitly identifies a BlackHawk v2.3 issue:
the PYTHIA hadronization table generator has:
Emax_init = 100000
so the maximum primary parton energy is 10^5 GeV.
The paper states that BlackHawk by default should not be used for very energetic meson fluxes above that scale.
It further states that its BlackHawk meson curves are not extended below approximately 1e9 g because the authors could not obtain results unaffected by this parton-energy limitation.
This is direct source-native support for the source-admission boundary.

13. RAW LOWER-MASS RUN VERSUS SOURCE-VALID REGENERATION
Define:
E2a / RAW_EXECUTOR_REGENERATION:
Run the existing public BlackHawk v2.3 BBN pipeline at M<1e9 g with its current hadronization tables.
This is technically runnable in the software interface.
But if the required primary energies exceed table support, BlackHawk uses hadronization extrapolation and/or an energy-limited representation.
E2a is therefore:
EXECUTOR DIAGNOSTIC.
It is NOT:
SOURCE-VALID E2.
Define:
E2b / SOURCE-SUPPORT-MATCHED REGENERATION:
Regenerate species rates using hadronization information that is physically supported over the actual primary-energy range needed for that PBH mass.
E2b is the object required for physical source admission.

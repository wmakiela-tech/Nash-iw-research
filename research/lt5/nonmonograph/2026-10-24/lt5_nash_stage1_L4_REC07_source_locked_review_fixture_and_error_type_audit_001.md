Let w=(h−ell)/2, c=(h+ell)/2, e=min(w,delta/s). Both actor protocols have bounded adversarial measurement noise, early u0 committed before the measurement, and fixed separate early/late actuation magnitude capacities B0,B1.

Original author LT5:
  J_two = max[e, w+max(|c|−B0,0)−B1].
  J_late = max[e, w+|c|−B0−B1].

REC10 subsequently derived:
  J_two − J_late
    = min[max(B0−|c|,0), max(w−B1−e,0)].

Proof: when B0≤|c| the maxima have the same non-floor argument, so the gap is zero. When B0>|c|, let q=B0−|c|>0 and a=w−B1, giving J_two=max(e,a), J_late=max(e,a−q). Their difference is min(q,max(a−e,0)). No assumption that both candidates inside max are positive is needed.

## Scientific mandate of the accepted direction

Study *empirically* how multi-agent scientific critique and independent provenance/execution checks change the correctness, appropriate restraint, and correction history of research claims. Treat true scientific errors, benign theorem sharpening, source-semantic clarifications, and justified physical HOLD as different outcomes. Do not turn five hand-picked traceable repairs into a study claiming observed efficacy. Retain failures, null outcomes and reviewer-induced mistakes.

The scientific priority is an independently adjudicable, reproducible paper outside the LT monograph. Its candidate research question is: at controlled evidence and budget, do independent source-verified/executable review protocols reduce incorrect scientific ACCEPT relative to self-review and text-only peer review without creating excessive false REJECT? Existing longitudinal NASH cases motivate hypotheses but cannot identify this causal effect.

## Stage 1 — work authorized by direction acceptance

1. **Sampling frame, not cherry-picked showcase.** Construct a fixed-scope list of candidate claims as atomic statements with source SHA/URL, first timestamp, question/source type, owner and material reviewed/not-reviewed status. Keep the five validated examples C01–C05 as methodological calibration, not the confirmatory analysis set. Use KN11's *fixed* Git comparison (99 commits, 58 matching research markdown paths) only as a custody boundary, not as a claim count or a complete October corpus.
2. **Two-level claim schema.** Separate a claim from the file/message in which it appears; mark formal quantifiers, predicted observable, primary-code execution, versioned source, mathematically meaningful outcome and status/authority. One document may contain several claims, and one claim several revisions.
3. **Evidence chain and independence.** For each claim, preserve claim-first snapshot, independent external source/code, next reviewer statement, and actual owner adoption or non-adoption. Mark inaccessible evidence *missing*, not inferred PASS.
4. **Blind rubric draft.** Define VALID / INVALID / INDETERMINATE / SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE, measured at frozen evidence state, and acceptance behavior ACCEPT / REJECT / HOLD. Reviewer-blind adjudication by separate domain specialists remains to be done.
5. **Pre-registration draft.** Planned comparison: arm A self-review, B independent text-only critique, C independent source/pinned-code/execution audit, matched time/compute/tool budget and source opportunity. Primary outcome false ACCEPT on truly erroneous claims; co-primary false REJECT of valid claims; secondary appropriate HOLD, material correction latency, reproducibility and research cost. Pilot feasibility and power calculation required before sample size lock.
6. **Independence and ethics.** Require paired/blocked problem-family design with sealed source snapshots and cross-arm information isolation; never treat multiple commits/revisions as independent trials. No private Slack, Drive or BW material released without separate permission and minimization.

Stage 1 may produce a read-only frozen *public-safe sample manifest*, a draft codebook, an adjudication rubric, and a pre-registration document. These are research preparations, not launch of a human-subject study or automated experiment.

## Conditions for Stage 2 trial

Before any controlled three-arm trial is called an *experiment performed*:
- Freeze claim corpus prior to treatment, including correct, incorrect and uncertain cases; establish independent ground truth for objectively adjudicable cases, with explicit unresolved status for physical conjectures.
- Obtain specific approval for any required new data access, outside reviewer involvement and external public release.
- Determine final sample size by a declared power calculation and reliable clustered units, not a cosmetic 'N=58'.
- Keep test arms isolated from their own outcomes and known full NASH discussion history; disclose model versions.
- Predefine failure criteria and reporting of negative/null effects.
- Publish/disseminate only after explicit publication and privacy review.

## 1. Exact research problem

We distinguish four properties: (S) archival source custody, (E) scientific claim-evidence grounding, (R) independent source/proof review, and (I) substantive correction or validated uptake by a receiver. S does NOT imply E; E does NOT imply R; Slack dispatch does NOT imply I; and I does NOT imply the thesis is novel or physically accepted.

Hypothesis for a FUTURE controlled study, not a finding from the archive:
  H1: at matched task/evidence/compute budgets, independent source-and-execution review reduces the proportion of material errors falsely accepted in technical claims compared with self-review or text-only independent critique.
  H2: it increases calibrated abstentions/HOLD where primary sources are missing, instead of fabricating closure.
  H3: its gain may depend strongly on source type (proof, numerical script, versioned article/code, physical simulator).
Null: no difference at matched budgets. Negative case: added review can cause fresh errors or false HOLD. The latter MUST be counted.

### Why this is distinct from LT
LT Ch1–7 studies exact operator reduction, target fidelity, Hilbert complexes, quantum instruments, kinetic target bounds, and cross-description physical consistency. This paper instead measures a social/computational research process: citation genealogy, claim revisions, correctness adjudication, and experimental arm effectiveness. Its data may come from LT but its outcome is NOT a dynamical observable of a physical model. Do not misappropriate LT theorem numbering or claim the project proves its own superiority.

## 4. Coding instructions for actual empirical corpus

Unit of analysis: one *atomic scientific claim* with exact source assertion, stated quantifiers, physical/mathematical carrier, declared target, first public timestamp and exact blob. An article may contain many distinct claims; one claim may have many files. An author claim and reviewer claim require separate records. Source code is separate from paper text. Multiple versions must be linked, not merged. A short summary may lack the full proof and must NOT be upgraded by presumed unavailable attachments.

Fields per unit:
  claim_id; episode_id; lineage; first_statement_verbatim_or_precise_paraphrase; mathematical_physical_carrier; quantifiers; target; earliest timestamp/ref/blob; source_accessible yes/no/partial; source_kind primary/code/numeric/secondary; reported_validation; independently_executable_code yes/no; review_independence same-cell/other-cell/external-human/unknown; review_action pass/sharpen/repair/scope_hold/no_material_change; receiver_action accepted_in_working_draft/executed_fix/rejected/hold/unknown; authority owner/reviewer/moderator; final_decision; missing_evidence; negative_knowledge; later_supersession_link.

Separate TWO outcomes:
  (a) scientific correctness adjudicated blindly as correct / incorrect / indeterminate / untestable under source contract;
  (b) documented behavior of the project: correction actually adopted, staged only, passed independently, or transmitted only.
Never substitute (b) for (a). Record reviewer-created false rejections and apparent error discoveries that merely cite known host prior art.

A claim is a material scientific error only if it changes a theorem quantifier, counterexample, falsifier, functional, observational likelihood, legal CP property, source provenance, sign/conclusion, or physical applicability. A d+2 valid but nonsharp proof is a precision gain, NOT automatically error. A proposed false physical bound never promoted to a claim is a successfully maintained HOLD, NOT a retracted result.

## 5. Scientific test: why a positive observational audit cannot prove an effect

For claim i define potential outcomes Y_i(0),Y_i(1) where Y=1 means material error incorrectly accepted, 0 means not; assignment A_i=0 conventional independent text review, A_i=1 source+proof executable review. Only Y_i(A_i) is observed. Existing traces preferentially document claims for which critiques were generated or regarded as important; A is not randomized and the missing potential outcome is unknown. There exist two data-generating worlds with EXACT identical archived Y_i(A_i): in one world Y_i(0)=Y_i(1) (zero causal effect); in another Y_i(1)<Y_i(0) for the exposed claims (positive benefit). Without extra assumptions both fit the records. Therefore the causal effect E[Y(1)-Y(0)] is NOT identified from the five exemplars or a 58-path archive census. This is ordinary potential-outcomes host mathematics, not a NASH theorem.

A retrospective **descriptive** study can still quantify fidelity of citations, status semantics, source coverage, and time-to-documented-correction within its selection frame. It must explicitly report missing inaccessible Slack/Drive/archived model traces, changing model versions and dependence among agent observations.

## 6. Prospective experimental protocol — NOT yet performed

- Source bank: freeze first snapshots for technically diverse problems, including valid claims, seeded subtle errors AND naturally disputed claims whose correctness can be adjudicated by independent experts. Pre-register source inclusion before assigning experimental arms. Stratify mathematical, code, source version, applied simulation, and inverse problem types; group by original problem family to limit near-duplicate leakage.
- Arms run on identical frozen evidence with matched available research tokens/time/tools and without cross-arm memory:
  A: original generator with independent SELF-REVIEW only;
  B: other model textual referee, no code/source executions beyond identical initial material;
  C: independent source-pinned, executable verification with reference/claim graph and explicit source-HOLD semantics.
- Pair designs across claims are permitted only if each arm is isolated and the evaluator ground truth is created independently. Consider ~40–60 distinct claims ×3 isolated arms as a feasibility target, NOT a statistically powered sample size; plan power and preregister the true confirmatory N after blind pilot variance estimation, with no outcome-dependent cherry-pick.
- Blind adjudication: at least two domain-trained adjudicators who do not know arm or NASH owner, reconcile mathematical correctness, and treat contested physical claims as INDETERMINATE. Explicit disagreement protocol and inter-rater agreement; external primary-source checks.
- Primary outcome: rate of incorrect ACCEPT decisions on independently adjudicated erroneous claims. Co-primary safety outcome: unjustified REJECT/HOLD of a correct claim. HOLD of unresolvable physics must be scored appropriately, not automatically counted as indecision error.
- Secondary: complete correct source chains, time/cost to materially valid correction, calibration of confidence vs adjudication, reproducible reruns, and false reviewer errors. Report denominators per claim type and cluster confidence intervals by problem family; never use commits/messages as independent Bernoulli observations.
- Prevent interference: same task input, frozen model settings, costs, no disclosure of a previous correction to the tested arm, no feedback into the original research corpus until trial completed. Avoid using NASH authors as blinded external referees on their own cases.
- Ethics/privacy: only public-safe primary sources in any external article; sensitive/private Slack/Drive/BW content excluded unless separately approved and properly anonymized. Do not expose personal email IDs or private owner contacts in public datasets. User/Moderator controls public publication.

Falsifier: if C does not reduce erroneous ACCEPT at matched costs, or increases false REJECT sufficiently to worsen balanced scientific decision quality, H1 fails. A successful NASH retrospective history alone cannot satisfy H1. The controlled study should publish negative/null results and any failures discovered in source anchoring.

## 1. Project achievement inventory — already established in public source bodies

**LT4 / hidden initial preparation versus generated memory.**
A source block generator L=[[A,B],[C,D]] with bounded off-diagonal couplings induces the hidden-preparation target response M(t)=GP S(t)J_Y and the distinct reduced dynamics kernel K(t)=B S_D(t) C. These maps have different types. LT4 proves a Duhamel-based relation between generated memory error and the history of M(t), and exact scalar-target minimax identities under a Hilbert hidden-state ball. It also supplies explicit endpoint-versus-history controls and scope guards for unbounded generators.
Public source:
https://github.com/wmakiela-tech/Nash-iw-research/blob/main/research/lt4/2026-10-18/lt4_exact_target_memory_prep_bridge_v1.md

**LT4 / target minimax and history Gramian.**
For bounded L in a Hilbert setting, target uncertainty due to y0 in a radius-R ball is R||M_T||. Whole-history uncertainty is measured by the operator from y0 to L²([0,T];Z), with Gramian W_T=∫ M_t* M_t dt. M(T)=0 and W_T=0 are different statements. The local Taylor coefficients GP L^k J_Y determine the response when the full generator is bounded (norm-analytic exponential); they do not give such a criterion for arbitrary C0 generators.
Public source:
https://github.com/wmakiela-tech/Nash-iw-research/blob/main/research/lt4/2026-10-17/lt4_hidden_state_target_minimax_gramian_001.md

**MT13 / target-relative optimal sensors.**
For finite-dimensional Hilbert y0 and exact free rank-k noiseless linear sensors, worst-case error can be reduced to R sigma_(k+1)(M). Under a fixed accessible channel C, every permitted observation L=H C retains an irreducible floor R||M|ker C||; ideal mathematical sensors must not be called physically available without an instrument contract. This is classical optimal recovery/SVD; MT13 does not claim a stochastic-risk, full-process, or quantum result.
Public source:
https://github.com/wmakiela-tech/Nash-iw-research/blob/main/research/mt13/20261018_hidden_state_target_optimal_sensors_singular_values.md

**MT13 / Banach quotient semigroup positive case.**
If r:X→Y is a bounded surjection of Banach spaces, U_t is C0 and ker r is invariant, then the induced quotient evolution V_t is automatically a C0 semigroup on Y; the generator requires a domain relation. Mere set-theoretic factorization lacks this conclusion without these topological hypotheses.
Public source:
https://github.com/wmakiela-tech/Nash-iw-research/blob/main/research/mt13/20261017_ch7_quotient_c0_semigroup_regularity.md

**PRF11 / distributional evidence is not deterministic target information.**
The previous public consolidation separates equality of trajectory supports, equality of probability laws, law-level identifiability, finite-sample decision risk, and intervention-conditioned history sufficiency. Finite Fourier-moment matching need not imply TV law closeness. A common circular heat kernel gives one *conditional* moment-to-TV repair for smoothed observation laws; this does not upgrade the unsmoothed process or confer physical instrument legality.

## 2. Four mature theory families and their *different* objects

### A. C0 semigroups / observation and control

Native object: a strongly continuous semigroup S(t) on a Banach/Hilbert carrier with generator A and appropriate domain. Operator observation and control maps require admissibility in the unbounded setting.

Mature backbone:
- Engel & Nagel, *One-Parameter Semigroups for Linear Evolution Equations*, GTM 194, Springer (2000), DOI 10.1007/b97696.
- Tucsnak & Weiss, *Observation and Control for Operator Semigroups*, Birkhäuser (2009), DOI 10.1007/978-3-7643-8994-9. Publisher TOC includes semigroups, control/observation operators, admissibility, observability, hyperbolic/parabolic cases.

Exact boundaries for project transfer:
(1) bounded-generator operator-norm analyticity is **not** a universal property of C0 semigroups;
(2) a point-output M(T) is not a history observability operator;
(3) ordinary structural observability is not by itself a noisy finite-sample risk statement;
(4) a Banach quotient result does not imply equivalent hidden microscopic carriers.

### B. Projection, hidden return and memory

Native object: exact reduced equations involving a memory kernel and an initial hidden-state/forcing term. Formal elimination does not justify discarding either.

Mature backbone:
- Chorin, Hald & Kupferman, *Optimal prediction with memory*, Physica D 166 (2002), 239–257, DOI 10.1016/S0167-2789(02)00446-3.
- Traditional Mori–Zwanzig and Zwanzig/Grabert projection methods; no claim that an arbitrary block-operator "return channel" is literally a memory term without time evolution and an admissible projection.

Important separation: a finite-dimensional LTI realization has rational transfer behavior (after declaring input/output and initial conditions); broad continuous-memory descriptions need not. Approximating a kernel does not by itself control a target unless initial-memory terms and solution stability are also bounded.

### C. Optimal recovery and operator widths

Native object: M:Y→Z, uncertainty class of hidden y, allowed observation L, target error norm, and a specified sensor/noise budget.

Mature backbone:
- Classical Hilbert singular-value minimax / optimal recovery.
- Pietsch-style s-numbers, specifically Gelfand numbers c_n(M)=inf_{codim V<n}||M|V||; see Ullrich, *Inequalities between s-numbers*, Advances in Operator Theory 9, article 82 (2024), DOI 10.1007/s43036-024-00386-x, open HTML.

Formal warning: compact Hilbert maps admit a singular-value ladder, but bounded noncompact maps generally cannot be treated as if they had one. Free-sensor optimum is not the optimum under a legal source-readout channel C. Sensor access and geometric norm are load-bearing.

### D. Statistical experiments, randomization and decision risk

Native object: a parameter-indexed family {P_theta} of probability laws over one declared observation experiment, with decision/loss semantics.

Mature backbone:
- Torgersen, *Comparison of Statistical Experiments*, Cambridge (1991), DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511666353.
- Blackwell comparison, Le Cam deficiency; source-specific law-distance controls or legal parameter-independent randomization are required.

Nonmaps:
- Equal deterministic target paths or finitely many features do not automatically imply TV/deficiency comparison.
- An observation-law equivalence does not identify full physical carrier, interventional implementation, or hidden variables.
- A common observation kernel is a substantive assumption; different apparatus under different unknown parameters cannot be silently treated as one Blackwell garbling.

## 3. Why there is not one "moment problem"

At least two mathematically nonidentical uses of the word *moment* occur in the public project:

(1) **Dynamical/Taylor moments** GP L^k J_Y or BD^kC, tied to derivatives of operator semigroup responses near t=0. For bounded generators, the norm-convergent exponential expansion gives global analytic continuation of the response; arbitrary C0 evolution lacks this automatic guarantee.

(2) **Distributional/Fourier moments** \hat P(k)=∫exp(-ikx)dP(x) on the circle. The entire infinite Fourier family determines a probability measure on the compact circle, but any prescribed finite prefix can agree while the raw laws remain TV-far. A *specified common smoothing kernel* may yield a bound at the smoothed-law level only.

An analogy between (1) and (2) is useful for pedagogy, but not a theorem bridge; the completeness topology, test family, time/spectral parameter and continuation regularity differ.

## 4. Operational typology before selecting a theorem

For every actual NASH source candidate fix in this order:

SOURCE: model carrier, generator/evolution, domains, initial/preparation set, admissible couplings, stochastic or deterministic status.
ACCESS: legal sensor/instrument channel and postprocessing; the true data object (single measurement, full trajectory, law, sample, controlled process).
TARGET: scalar/vector endpoint, whole history, selected functional, law-level parameter, or decision under a declared loss.
APPROXIMATION: generator norm, output/transfer, kernel L1, trajectory error, likelihood energy, KL, TV, deficiency or other legal comparison.
QUANTIFIERS: fixed theta versus uniform theta; fixed y0 versus worst-case y0; single endpoint versus every admissible time/policy; exact observation versus noise and budget.

Only then identify a host theorem and check *every* hypothesis. Avoid changing any of these entries mid-proof to make a bound seem stronger.

## 5. Three discriminating nonmaps

**NONMAP 1: NOISELESS SENSOR WIDTH → NOISY DECISION RISK.**
R c_(k+1)(M) is a worst-case Hilbert recovery quantity with free exact linear sensors. It is not the Bayes/frequentist risk for noisy finite-sample observations unless a new source-specific experiment/loss construction is supplied.

**NONMAP 2: DETERMINISTIC MEMORY ERROR → STATISTICAL DEFICIENCY.**
Bounds on ||M(t)|| or ||K-K_m|| control an explicitly typed deterministic target; they do not imply a uniform TV bound for probabilistic observed laws without a stochastic model, common carrier and a legal likelihood/observation map.

**NONMAP 3: FINITE-DIMENSIONAL ANALYTICITY → GENERAL C0 MEMORY.**
All coefficients of a norm-analytic operator response may determine it, but C0 regularity alone does not make a Taylor jet complete. The exact hypothesis to transport is analyticity/quasianalytic uniqueness for the relevant observed response, not the word "semigroup" by itself.

## 6. Revised scientific priority (no new project gate)

Highest information value is in *source-native bridges* where the four theory families supply different and potentially incompatible hypotheses:
- Is the source readout an admissible bounded/noisy operator on the declared semigroup carrier?
- Can hidden-state and memory approximation be related to the **actual** target (not just a surrogate)?
- Does an available sensor class bound the irreducible target uncertainty?
- If a statistical decision conclusion is desired, has the source model produced a uniform law/likelihood-level certificate?

If existing mature theory resolves the case, classify as an absorbed application. If the physical source or legal observation is missing, classify as SOURCE_ADMISSION_UNKNOWN, not a theorem failure. No automatic transfer to H8R/QRF, Lorentzian form factors, kinetic cosmology or NCDG follows from this report.

The research question after this global pass is **not** to create a fifth abstract information-preservation formalism. It is to choose one scientifically admitted source in which the intersection of operator response, instrument-access constraint and decision/loss semantics creates a demonstrable, falsifiable difficulty after exact prior-art subtraction.

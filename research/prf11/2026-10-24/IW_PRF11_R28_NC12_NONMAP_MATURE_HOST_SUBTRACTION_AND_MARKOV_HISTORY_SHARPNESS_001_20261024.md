## 2. PRF11 mathematical delta: NC §3.10 has TV→1, not merely TV>0

NC ART006 constructs two Markov kernels on {0,1}, each with the SAME initial distribution (1/2,1/2):
  P_1=[[1/2,1/2],[1/2,1/2]],
  P_2=[[2/3,1/3],[1/3,2/3]].
Every n-step finite history has strictly positive probability under both. NC proved and computed differences at n=1,...,18, while explicitly NOT proving the n→∞ asymptotic.

For path X_0,...,X_n define switch indicators
  F_j=1_{X_j != X_{j−1}}, j=1,...,n.
For symmetric two-state Markov transitions, the F_j are IID Bernoulli(p) with p=1/2 for P1, p=1/3 for P2, independent of the initially uniform X0. The map (X0,F1,...,Fn) ↔ path is bijective. Therefore the path laws' total variation is EXACTLY the TV of independent Bernoulli sequences; the likelihood depends only on K=sum F_j, so the sufficient statistic K loses no TV and
  TV(path law1, path law2)
    = TV(Bin(n,1/2), Bin(n,1/3))
    = (1/2)Σ_{k=0}^n C(n,k)
           |2^{−n} − (1/3)^k(2/3)^{n−k}|.           (R28.1)

Let E_n={K/n>5/12}. Hoeffding with midpoint separation 1/12 gives
  P_1(E_n^c) ≤ exp(−n/72),
  P_2(E_n)   ≤ exp(−n/72),
and thus
  TV(path law1,path law2) ≥1−2exp(−n/72) → 1.          (R28.2)

Since TV≤1, convergence is exact. In fact by strong law the infinite-path laws live on disjoint almost-sure frequency events (F mean 1/2 vs 1/3), so they are mutually singular as probability measures on full infinite histories. **They nonetheless have the same support on every finite cylinder, and indeed positive mass on every finite binary path.** Do not equate "same finite positive-support histories" with equal infinite path measure.

Proof is elementary mature IID concentration. It sharpens NC's chosen witness IN ITS OWN CARRIER, not a general assertion that all unequal path distributions become singular.

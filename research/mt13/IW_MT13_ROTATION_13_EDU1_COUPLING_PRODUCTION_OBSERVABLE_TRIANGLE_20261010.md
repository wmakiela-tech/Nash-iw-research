## 2. Scientific question evolution
Original learner question: does dark matter evade detection because it has very few relations with ordinary matter, possibly belonging to a different relational/spacetime sector?

After foundation and referee: do NOT erase relation architecture by replacing the question with a generic observational-equivalence slogan. The proper source-native question is:
WHICH TYPED COUPLING ARCHITECTURES + PRODUCTION HISTORIES CAN GENERATE AN ADMITTED DARK-MATTER POPULATION AND THE JOINT OBSERVED GRAVITATIONAL RESPONSE, WHILE FORCING A NONDEGENERATE NEW OBSERVABLE?

Relation count alone is neither physical invariant nor observability order. A weak force and a strong force count equally under naive counting; destructive interference and decoupling break monotonicity. Internal dark interactions can be rich when visible portals vanish.

## 3. Three interacting structures, not a four-bin taxonomy
C — coupling/response: universality and charge direction; force range and propagation; self-interaction; geometry/metric/brane transport.
P — production history: gravitational creation, inflaton decay, hidden reheating, hidden-sector thermal freeze-out, freeze-in, misalignment; primordial perturbations and initial conditions.
O — observational family: abundance, CMB/isocurvature, LSS/free-streaming, halo and cluster structure, lensing, EP/composition tests, fifth-force/range tests, GW propagation and extra modes.

The actual constraint is joint feasibility:
For candidate model theta and allowed histories h in H(theta), require P(theta,h) produces an admissible abundance and initial state, and the evolved response O(theta,h) lies in the admitted observational region. A model cannot be promoted by fitting only one marginal response with independently refitted nuisance histories. This is a general modeling prescription, not a new theorem.

## 4. Exact guards from Mech and Rec9
- A composition-dependent fifth force can distinguish a specified charge coupling from universal metric gravity; it does NOT identify H1 vs H2 labels. H1 with zero portal and strict H2 can both show universal response.
- Mass-proportional coupling can be composition-blind while altering range/strength. Composition axis and range/strength axis are distinct.
- A normalized nonzero charge-direction shape may not depend on coupling amplitude; experimental signal-to-noise vanishes as amplitude tends to zero. At zero amplitude the normalized direction is undefined.
- Hidden-sector thermal relics are mature counterexamples to 'no SM coupling implies no thermal freeze-out'. Internal dark thermalization at its own temperature is different from thermal equilibrium with the Standard Model.
- Garcia–Pierre–Verner 2023 spectator-scalar/isocurvature restrictions are source-specific, not an exclusion of all gravitational-only dark matter.
- Different branes may live in one higher-dimensional bulk; different metrics need not mean unrelated spacetimes.
- H1/H2/H3/H4 are overlapping descriptive regions, not a partition. An H3 decoupling limit may reproduce H1/H2 low-energy response. Modified gravity, hidden carrier and emergent force can coexist in hybrid models.

## 5. A scientific discriminating protocol (existing data first)
Choose TWO fully specified competing models, rather than broad ontology labels.
Freeze (a) action/dynamics, (b) coupling types and magnitudes, (c) production mechanism, (d) initial conditions and common nuisance freedoms, (e) target observable family.
Demand a joint fit to already available cosmological/astrophysical data; no context-specific arbitrary retuning.
Seek one predeclared response for which their admitted predicted sets are disjoint, or show they remain overlapping at current precision.
Possible axes: a concrete composition charge direction; a concrete fifth-force range; a model-specific isocurvature signature; a halo self-interaction law; a specific KK/radion/GW propagation signature; a frozen multi-probe constitutive-gravity relation.
A negative portal search never proves strict gravity-only. A single positive composition test identifies a coupling pattern only under an admitted source model. No universal test identifies 'another spacetime'.

## 6. New synthesis / falsifiable research question
Can two models be constructed with indistinguishable calibrated low-redshift gravitational response, yet different production-history restrictions that predict distinguishable primordial isocurvature or small-scale transfer functions without changing the allowed late-time nuisance contract?

This is a bounded model-pair design question, not a novelty claim. A model-specific calculation must precede any empirical claim. If existing model families already provide such pairs, classify as mature-host demonstration; if no pair survives shared calibration, stop.

## 7. Learner-facing answer
The most valuable intuition is not that dark matter must be outside our spacetime, but that different types of connection to visible matter determine what can be measured. The dark sector may be almost invisible electromagnetically while still gravitating, interacting strongly with itself, and possessing a distinct cosmological formation history. Some extra-dimensional or relational models are possible, but to turn them into science we must specify a mechanism and one observable consequence that competing models cannot imitate.

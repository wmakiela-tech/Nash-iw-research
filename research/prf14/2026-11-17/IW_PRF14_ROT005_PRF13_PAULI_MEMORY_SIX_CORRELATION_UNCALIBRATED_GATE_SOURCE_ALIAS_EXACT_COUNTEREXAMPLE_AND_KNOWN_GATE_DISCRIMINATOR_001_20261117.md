## 1. Frozen legal source and observable

Label Pauli unitaries \`U_(x,z)=X^x Z^z\` by \`v,w∈G=F_2²\`, and set \`chi_X(x,z)=(-1)^z\`, \`chi_Y(x,z)=(-1)^(x+z)\`, \`chi_Z(x,z)=(-1)^x\`. For each *exogenous* joint classical noise distribution \`p(v,w)>=0\`, \`Σ p=1\`, a two-step experiment uses one first random Pauli, then a fixed deterministic unitary \`V\`, then one last random Pauli. The pair \`v,w\` is drawn before the control and does not depend on the control choice; mixtures of unitary channels are CPTP and compatible with causal two-step comb constraints.

Define \`C_(j,i)(p) = Σ_v,w p(v,w) chi_j(v)chi_i(w)\` for Pauli \`j,i∈{X,Y,Z}\`. By the original predecessor exact identity:

\`T_ij(p;V) = R_ij(V)*C_(j,i)(p)\`,

where \`R(V)∈SO(3)\` is the 3×3 Bloch rotation of \`V\`. Since every branch is unitary and unital, the **complete externally accessible effective single-qubit channel with the same fixed inserted V and no intermediate recorded outcome** is determined by \`T\`; even using an ancilla only *outside* the sandwich does not help distinguish equal effective CPTP maps.

Ten other original Fourier coordinates \`C_a0,C_0b,C_cc\` determine the isolated first noise, isolated second noise, and idle/no-intervention composition. They are the original PRF13 ordinary control channels, not complete process tomography.

## 2. Exact two-world construction: six nonzero source coordinates on BOTH sides

Use two **different** already licensed physical qubit-unitary Bloch rotations, both drawn unchanged from the predecessor:

\`R_star = (1/3)[[-1,2,2],[2,-1,2],[2,2,-1]]\`, arising from \`Vstar=-i(X+Y+Z)/sqrt3\`;

\`R_old  = (1/7)[[-3,-2,6],[6,-3,2],[2,6,3]]\`, arising from \`Vold=(I-i(X+Y+2Z))/sqrt7\`.

Both have \`R^T R=I\` and \`det R=1\` over exact rationals; they are distinct legal rotations. Let \`j\` denote the input Pauli coordinate and \`i\` the final one. Choose the six off-diagonal source Fourier coefficients:

\`c_star(j,i) = 1/20\`, for every \`j≠i\`;

\`c_old(j,i) = 1/[30 R_old[i,j]]\`, for every \`j≠i\`.

Explicitly, \`c_old\` takes three possible nonzero values \`−7/60, 7/60, 7/180\`, according to \`R_old[i,j]=−2/7,2/7,6/7\`. Its six absolute-value sum is \`7/15\`; for \`c_star\` the six absolute-value sum is \`3/10\`. Thus positivity is immediate even without enumeration by Fourier inversion:

\`p_k(v,w) = [1 + Σ_{j≠i} c_k(j,i) chi_j(v)chi_i(w)]/16\` for \`k∈{star,old}\`.

All 16 probabilities in both distributions are strictly positive. The independent \`Fraction\` program enumerates them, finding the exact minima

\`min p_star = 9/160\`, \`min p_old = 11/288\`,

and \`Σ p_k =1\`. Both stage marginals are uniform \`1/4\), and both have every idle-endpoint diagonal Fourier coefficient \`C_(a,a)=0\` for \`a=X,Y,Z\`. Thus their first-stage, second-stage and idle-endpoint channels all equal complete qubit depolarization, just as in PRF13.

They are **genuinely distinct source distributions**, with exact classical total-variation distance

\`TV(p_star,p_old)= (1/2) Σ_v,w |p_star(v,w)-p_old(v,w)| = 1/12\`.

More strongly, **every one** of the six hidden off-diagonal source correlations is nonzero in both models, unlike a trivial all-zero source or a single-coordinate witness.

## 3. Theorem — identical complete effective channel despite distinct source memory and gate

For \`i≠j\`, the first world satisfies

\`T_star[i,j] = (2/3)*(1/20)=1/30\`.

The second world satisfies, by its exact definition,

\`T_old[i,j] = R_old[i,j]*[1/(30 R_old[i,j])] = 1/30\`.

For \`i=j\`, both sources have \`C_(j,i)=0\`, so \`T_star[i,i]=T_old[i,i]=0\`.

Consequently

\`T_star = T_old = (1/30)[[0,1,1],[1,0,1],[1,1,0]]\`.

Together with trace preservation and unitality, this identifies the **entire** single-qubit CPTP map accessible with a single fixed but uncalibrated inserted unitary. Hence every input state / final qubit POVM outside that sandwich yields the SAME outcome distribution; the equality persists for ancillas that interact only before and after the entire effective channel. There is no success probability exceeding random guessing for binary equal-prior discrimination restricted to this one experimental interface, even with arbitrarily many repetitions of it. This does NOT contradict ROT010's optimal comb tester with a separate middle slot, which is a different, richer intervention class.

But the underlying source noise distributions have TV=1/12 and the unknown actual inserted gates differ as physical unitaries. Therefore the inverse map \`(p,V) -> (ordinary step/endpoint channels, T(p;V))\` is **not injective** on the PRF13 source family once V is unknown, even with every one of the six coordinates nonzero. Both models are physically CPTP mixtures; this is a source-specific, exactly checked observational alias.

**Proof scope**: the six settings of ROT012, and indeed arbitrary process tomography treating the fixed sandwich \`noise_2 ∘ Ad_V ∘ noise_1\` as ONE input-output channel. It is NOT equality of process tensors for all possible intermediate quantum instruments. Known V is still a sufficient mathematical condition for source reconstruction via the predecessor's identities; we have not challenged its theorem.

## 4. Cheapest legal discriminator after ONE independent control is truly known

A method that can insert a SECOND, **independently known, calibrated common gate** \`W=Vstar\` in either world, replacing the originally uncalibrated inserted V, accesses the source difference. Prepare the +Y eigenstate, apply both exogenous source Pauli steps with W between them, and measure X at the end.

For \`i=X,j=Y\`, \`R_star[X,Y]=2/3\`; the two source coefficients are \`c_star(Y,X)=1/20\` and \`c_old(Y,X)=−7/60\`. Therefore

\`Delta T_(X,Y) = (2/3)[1/20−(−7/60)] = 1/9\`.

The final binary ±X measurement has exactly

\`TV((+X,-X)_star,(+X,-X)_old)=|Delta T|/2 = 1/18\`.

This is a **source-specific one-setting positive control**, not a general identifiability theorem for arbitrary unknown p and V. It requires truly independent knowledge of W and the same exogenous source law across both interventions. If an experiment merely *calls* another imperfect control W by the same label, its calibration cannot be assumed, and the ambiguity can remain. Alternatively, a separate gate bypass/calibration sequence may distinguish Rstar and Rold directly, but that is additional experimental access beyond the original six settings.

## 2. Candidate D1: independence of scientific evidence under correlated AI reviewers — TOP RELATIVE PRIORITY

**Working question, not established novelty:**
When a multi-model research team generates/referees a mathematical or empirical claim, which shared exposures (model lineage, retrieved papers, intermediate solutions, summary handoffs, persuasive messages, source errors) cause *correlated false confirmations*, and under what intervention does blind independent source-native verification reduce critical false-acceptance while preserving verified true discoveries at a matched overall compute/human budget?

This is NOT 'more agent debates'. It is a pre-specified causal question about an EVIDENCE-SOURCE DEPENDENCE NETWORK, objective scientific errors and the marginal effectiveness of independent verification. Differentiate (a) independent model name/family, (b) independent random sampling, (c) INDEPENDENT primary source accession, (d) reviewer who did not see author conclusion, (e) SOURCE-NATIVE execution/proof check. Correlation of errors alone is not causal evidence of source dependence; task difficulty, model strength and prior exposure confound it.

Minimal scientific design: randomize blocks of source-native scientific tasks with independently secured ground truth among high-capability single agent, parallel ensemble with standardized synthesis, interacting debate, and blinded source-native adversarial referee variants; on formally approved NASH C1, preserve its exact THREE-ARM primary design (single vs independent parallel vs NASH custody) rather than unilaterally modifying it. Additional factorial exposure/ablations require fresh Moderator authorization and separate prereg. Factor in task family, model family, same-paper overlap, shared intermediate solutions, blind source access and overall budget. A planted FALSE proof claim, known false citation and genuinely correct obscure result are calibration positive/negative controls, not real-world misinformation injections.

Outcome: PRIMARY externally adjudicated proportion of task claims fully correct AND substantive/novelty-faithful under equal resources, including false declaration of a wrong result 'independently confirmed'; SECONDARY false alarms, missed defects, verified hypothesis diversity and accepted true novel conclusions, reviewer time, money, tools and human minutes. Data must be source-native, with masked referees who have no answer exposure, and a frozen independent holdout. The old NASH logs are NOT randomized proof; use only to construct possible error taxonomies, do not train/test on reused tasks and then claim superiority.

Potential doctoral theorem or contribution ABOVE nearest hosts, still OPEN: an identifiable/corrected estimator for 'effective independent confirmation' conditional on evidence provenance and an experimentally validated intervention mechanism decreasing false scientific acceptance at equal cost, including models of correlated exposure and explicit failure modes. Simple design-effect formulas for correlated Bernoulli votes, independent reviewer rules and blind auditing are mature; no novelty unless a real new result or strong causal scientific-task evidence beyond published studies.

GO after complete near-host source review ONLY if (i) non-obvious causal effect under preregistered task isolation with holdout, (ii) robust against matched-budget majority/self-consistency and FalsifyAgent baselines, (iii) substantially source-native/novelty-faithful verified end outcomes, and (iv) independent external expert review. STOP if merely reproduces known diversity/ensembling or performance disappears at matched budgets. No claimed current empirical evidence that NASH wins.

## 3. Candidate D2: causally maintaining epistemic diversity and falsifiable null hypotheses in AI-led research

**Working question:** What controlled protocol of deliberate source diversification, adversarial null-hypothesis generation, model-family separation and hypothesis-portfolio exploration increases REAL verified scientific coverage (novel validated hypotheses, correct negative hypotheses and distant domain connections) WITHOUT increasing uncorrected false discoveries at a fixed budget?

Crucial mature near hosts: Nature 2026 41.3m science-focus contraction observational; arXiv:2606.08251 extensive scientific human rating of ideas and convergence/null-hypothesis failure; Nature Co-Scientist 2026 generate/critic/evolution and scientific experiments; multiagent debate diversity matched baseline; ScienceAgentBench scientific outputs.

Nontrivial new empirical design would manipulate source-access topology and active divergence protocol across blinded scientific problems with pre-declared novelty/validity scoring, rather than compare cosmetic persona prompts. Randomize multiple teams to independent literature niches versus shared top-ranked corpus, force explicit null/inversion claims versus free exploration, control human moderator budget, and evaluate outputs using separate novelty source-harvest and scientific proof/external validation. Score portfolio metrics with BOTH validity and distance; avoid maximizing raw textual embedding diversity which rewards useless hallucinations.

Potential impact high across philosophy of science, AI4Science, scientometrics. Experimental feasibility moderate if original questions in matched fields accessible; independent domain experts and genuinely out-of-sample scientific outcomes costly. Risk novelty medium-high: many AI creativity/portfolio and source diversification studies exist. Strongest open gap is CAUSAL mechanism under scientific outcome verification, not fact that AI can homogenize ideas.

## 4. Candidate D3: durable NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE during autonomous scientific team succession

**Working question:** How should a research system maintain and transfer *withdrawn propositions, failed independent reviews, incompatible sources, disconfirmed hypotheses, and exact reason-for-STOP* so that subsequent agent successors avoid resurrecting known false scientific claims WITHOUT losing useful alternative hypotheses and creativity?

NASH succession and negative-knowledge custody make available authentic real-world error taxonomy, but the unrandomized previous log alone cannot estimate effects. Existing memory benchmarks LongMINT, stale-fact updates, 2026 six-month persistent research fleet and differentiated memory may already encompass basic designs. NO novelty for simply keeping metadata, provenance or exception logs.

A novel controlled benchmark would create source-native multi-week research trajectories with equal-length raw chat, ordinary summary, claim-status/negative-evidence ledger, and fact-level provenance-linked successor packets. Inject known correction and later ambiguous update AFTER a claim had been previously refuted (entire task frozen). Evaluate (i) reanimation rate of withdrawn hypotheses, (ii) correct use of current source, (iii) retention of actually valid alternative leads, (iv) independent source verification quality, and (v) human supervision and cost at fixed model context. Distinguish stale memory from independent scientific claim status. Can include cross-family blinded successors.

GO only for important experimentally demonstrated causal improvement plus a principled state transition representation or real source-native scientific truth generalization beyond existing benchmarks. STOP if only recapitulates ordinary memory update/forgetting results. High NASH operational applicability; external novelty and field-level value uncertain.

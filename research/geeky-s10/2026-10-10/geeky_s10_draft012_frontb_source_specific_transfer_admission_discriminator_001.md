4. Why donor compatibility is not source admission
The donor equations allow generic source terms, which makes PBH insertion architecturally plausible. But source-native admission additionally requires quantitative control of assumptions that are regime-dependent:
- fast hadron equilibration/depletion versus Hubble and source-variation timescales;
- terminal Hawking source acceleration and threshold opening;
- PBH/background backreaction;
- species/energy semantics of the Hawking/PYTHIA/HAZMA source;
- preservation of the joint (M_i,t_f) temporal carrier;
- no double counting across direct hadronic and network channels;
- target error on Y_p/D/H, not merely hadron-state or integrated-yield error.
These are not answered by saying that the source contract is well provenance-tagged. They are source-to-host transfer conditions.
5. Exact target-weighted obstruction
Mech-S6/MT13 provide an exact reason why a generic donor validation cannot be read directly as final-BBN target validation.
For prescribed hadron depletion Gamma and channel branching b_c=Gamma_c/Gamma, with dn/dt=S-Gamma n,
the integrated channel-yield error satisfies exactly:
Y_c^exact-Y_c^DE = -[b_c n] + integral dot(b_c) n dt.
Hence constant b_c plus vanishing endpoint population can make the UNWEIGHTED integrated channel yield exact even when state tracking is imperfect or S(t) varies rapidly.
But for a time-sensitive target with weight w(t):
T_c^exact-T_c^DE = -[w b_c n] + integral d(w b_c)/dt * n dt.
Therefore:
EXACT TOTAL CHANNEL COUNT != EXACT Y_p/DH.
The donor closure can pass a low-resolution integrated-yield criterion while failing a target-weighted BBN criterion if residual hadron population overlaps a time region where target sensitivity changes.
6. Feedback coupling adds another source-specific gate
The prescribed-Gamma identity is not enough for the actual state-coupled closure when Gamma depends on the evolving nucleon/hadron state.
At a fixed point F(n)=S/Gamma(n),
F'(n*)=-d ln Gamma/d ln n.
A bound |F'(n*)|<1 gives only local contraction for direct iteration. It is not a global existence/uniqueness theorem.
Therefore source admission for PBH/B2 also requires a feedback-coupled existence/conditioning certificate or direct validation in the source-matched regime. This is source-specific transfer validation, not provenance metadata.

5. THEORY FAMILY A — CONTROLLED MARKOV / STATE ABSTRACTION
5.1 State-dependent admissible actions are standard
Standard MDP formulations allow the set of actions available to the controller to depend on the current state:
A(x).
Therefore:
STATE-DEPENDENT EXPERIMENT AVAILABILITY
is not by itself a new mathematical object.
If the complete variable r controlling admissibility can be incorporated into a Markov-sufficient state x,
then the family E(r) becomes an ordinary state-dependent admissible action family A(x).

7. THEORY FAMILY C — QUANTUM COMBS, PROCESS TENSORS, SUPERPROCESSES
7.1 Quantum combs already provide a higher-order operational carrier
Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti (2009):

OPERATIONS ON OPERATIONS

7.2 Process tensors provide the multi-time operational process
Pollock et al. (2018):

The modern 2026 Keeling–Stoudenmire–Bañuls–Reichman Perspective confirms that process tensors are now a mature general framework for observable non-Markovian open dynamics.
7.3 Instrument dependence is already structural
Taranto et al. (2019):

PROPERTY DEPENDS ON TESTER / INSTRUMENT FAMILY

8. THEORY FAMILY D — QUANTUM REFERENCE FRAMES
Here the subtraction does NOT completely collapse.
8.1 Operational QRF transformations already transform quotient-level descriptions
Carette–Głowacki–Loveridge (2025):
QRF transformations are built around operational equivalence classes and framed/relative observables.
Invertibility depends on properties of the target frame, including localizability.
Thus:
FRAME-DEPENDENT OPERATIONAL QUOTIENTS
are already mature enough that PRF must not treat them as generic new structure.
8.2 Non-ideal temporal QRFs change measurement-event semantics
Hausmann–Schmidhuber–Castro-Ruiz (2025):
two measurement constructions that agree for ideal clocks become operationally distinct for finite-resource clocks.
In the dynamical/purified construction, non-ideal clocks can produce time-nonlocal dynamics, nonunitarity and limits on sharp event ordering.
Therefore:
SAME NOMINAL MEASUREMENT
need not mean
SAME OPERATIONAL EVENT ACROSS FRAME REGIMES.
8.3 Multi-clock causal consistency can require dynamic intervention embedding
Baumann–Lock (Phys. Rev. D, 5 Oct 2026):
for the studied relational dynamics, multiple clocks force a sharper distinction.
Only the approach dynamically incorporating interventions into the constraint yields a consistent causal picture across clocks; temporal delocalization then appears.
Thus:
INTERVENTION AS EXTERNAL LABEL
can be insufficient in a temporal-QRF problem.
8.4 Open-system reduced QRF channels exist, but are one-time objects
Luppi–Kabel–Giacomini–Smirne (arXiv:2607.05578):
reduced QRF channels describe accessible reduced dynamics after frame change and tracing inaccessible frame/environment degrees of freedom.
This absorbs:
QRF CHANGE + OPEN SYSTEM + INACCESSIBLE OLD FRAME
at the reduced-channel level.
But:
ONE-TIME REDUCED CHANNELS
do not automatically determine
A FULL MULTI-TIME PROCESS TENSOR.
8.5 Finite-resource frames can backreact under successive operations
Garmier–Hausmann–Castro-Ruiz (arXiv:2512.19343, current 2026):
non-ideal finite-resource QRF perspectives admit reversible transformations in their framework and successive relative operations can backreact on the QRF.
This sharpens the residual:

8.6 Composition is itself QRF-relative
Sahdo–Castro-Ruiz (arXiv:2608.23698):
subsystem composition in QRF perspectives is more constrained than ordinary tensor appending; not every state is compatible with naive tensor-product addition.
Therefore:
STATE COMPOSITION ACROSS QRFs
is already a recognized issue.
But:

!=

3. FOUNDATION FAMILY A — QUANTUM COMBS / HIGHER-ORDER MAPS
Chiribella, D'Ariano, Perinotti:
Quantum Circuit Architecture, PRL 101, 060401 (2008).
Theoretical Framework for Quantum Networks, PRA 80, 022339 (2009).
Core mature result:
quantum networks and admissible transformations of quantum networks are represented by quantum combs / higher-order maps.
The 2009 axiomatic result establishes a universality of quantum memory channels:
an admissible transformation of a quantum network can be physically realized as a suitable sequence of memory channels.
Therefore:
MEMORY
+
SEQUENTIAL HISTORY
+
AUXILIARY CARRIER
+
TRANSFORMATION OF NETWORKS
are NOT in themselves residual scientific structure.
A claim that finite-resource QRF backreaction forces “beyond higher-order maps” is not earned merely from the existence of backreaction.
4. FOUNDATION FAMILY B — PROCESS TENSORS
Pollock et al.,
Non-Markovian quantum processes: Complete framework and efficient characterization,
PRA 97, 012127 (2018).
Core:
a process tensor is an operational representation of an arbitrary multi-time open quantum process under interventions.
It contains the uncontrolled environment influence and temporal correlations while taking experimenter operations as inputs.
Thus:
OPEN SYSTEM
+
NON-MARKOVIAN MEMORY
+
MULTI-TIME INTERVENTIONS
is mature process-tensor territory.
Important subtraction:
a family of one-time channels does not determine a general non-Markovian process tensor.
Therefore:
REDUCED_QRF_CHANNEL
!=
MULTI_TIME_QRF_PROCESS
remains a legal distinction.
5. FOUNDATION FAMILY C — SUPERPROCESSES / AGENT CAPABILITY
Berk, Garner, Yadin, Modi, Pollock,
Resource theories of multi-time processes: A window into quantum non-Markovianity,
Quantum 5, 435 (2021).
Core:
multi-time processes can themselves be transformed by operationally implementable superprocesses.
The allowed transformation class depends on the agent's control/communication capability.
Therefore:
TESTER / CONTROL CAPABILITY
and
PROCESS TRANSFORMATION
already coexist in mature theory.
This absorbs the generic idea:
“the experiment family is restricted or capability-dependent, therefore a new higher structure is required.”
The residual must instead concern whether the QRF change can be represented as ONE LEGAL transformation between correctly typed multi-time operational objects.

7. FOUNDATION FAMILY E — OPERATIONAL QRF TRANSFORMATIONS
Carette, Głowacki, Loveridge,
Operational Quantum Reference Frame Transformations,
Quantum 9, 1680 (2025).
Source-level lesson:
QRF transformations can be formulated operationally on equivalence classes defined by accessible relative/framed observables.
Invertibility requires framework-specific conditions such as localizability of the final frame.
Therefore:
QRF transformation is not merely coordinate relabeling.
The relevant scientific objects can be quotient / accessibility typed.
But:
this result is primarily a state / observable / operational-equivalence framework.
It does not by itself provide a general transformation theorem for open non-Markovian process tensors.
8. FOUNDATION FAMILY F — NON-IDEAL FINITE-RESOURCE QRF
Garmier, Hausmann, Castro-Ruiz,
The Perspectives of Non-Ideal Quantum Reference Frames,
arXiv:2512.19343 (2025 preprint/current research).
Source-level result:
the construction extends QRF perspectives to finite-resource/non-ideal frames;
successive relational operations can backreact on the QRF.
Critical subtraction:
FRAME_BACKREACTION
does NOT imply
NO FIXED SUPERPROCESS.
Backreaction is exactly the kind of history dependence that can often be moved into an enlarged memory carrier.
The first mature-host move must therefore be:
INCLUDE THE FRAME RESOURCE / STATE HISTORY IN THE PROCESS CARRIER
and retry the higher-order representation.
9. FOUNDATION FAMILY G — TEMPORAL QRF EVENT SEMANTICS
Hausmann, Schmidhuber, Castro-Ruiz,
Measurement events relative to temporal quantum reference frames,
Quantum 9, 1616 (2025).
Source-level result:
two measurement constructions agree in ideal-clock regimes but become operationally distinct for non-ideal finite-resource clocks.
The dynamical/purified approach can produce time-nonlocal and nonunitary effective evolution and limitations on sharp event ordering.
Baumann, Lock,
Time delocalization and causality across temporal quantum reference frames,
Phys. Rev. D 114, 086005, published 5 October 2026.
Source-level result:
for multiple clocks, the studied consistent operational-causality picture requires dynamically embedding interventions in the constraint; temporal localization becomes frame-relative and can connect to indefinite causal order.
This is stronger than “frame state changes.”
The scientifically difficult object is now:
EVENT / INTERVENTION TYPING ACROSS PERSPECTIVES.
10. FOUNDATION FAMILY H — QRF COMPOSITION
Sahdo, Castro-Ruiz,
Compositionality in quantum reference frame perspectives,
arXiv:2608.23698 (2026 preprint).
Within their declared QRF formalism:
ordinary tensor-product appending is not the generic composition rule;
only compatible states may be appended in the direct way;
a different operation (“classicalisation”) has a different operational meaning.
Therefore:
STATE COMPOSITION COMPATIBILITY
is source-native and QRF-relative.
But this is not yet a theorem about full process / tester composition.
Thus:
STATE_COMPOSITION_RULE
!=
PROCESS_COMPOSITION_THEOREM.
11. FOUNDATION FAMILY I — OPEN QRF CHANNELS
Luppi, Kabel, Giacomini, Smirne,
Reduced Quantum-Reference-Frame Channels for Open Quantum Systems,
arXiv:2607.05578 (2026).
Source-level result:
after a QRF transformation, inaccessible old-frame/environment degrees can be traced out;
this motivates reduced QRF channels connecting one frame description to the accessible subsystem in another.
This absorbs:
OPEN SYSTEM + QRF CHANGE + REDUCED ACCESS.
But the object is a channel-level reduced dynamical description.
It does not supply:
a general transformation of an arbitrary multi-time process tensor plus its legal tester family.
12. FOUNDATION FAMILY J — PROCESS-MATRIX FRAME PERSPECTIVES
Apadula, Grinbaum, Brukner,
Frame perspectives for process matrices: from coordinate parametrization to spacetime representation,
arXiv:2604.02873 (2026).
For pure processes, causal-reference-frame and time-delocalized-subsystem descriptions are treated as coordinate parametrizations of a perspective-neutral higher-order object.
Two important positive results:
- perspective changes can be implemented while reshuffling past/future assignments;
- alternatively, extending the process with QRF subsystems can provide a shared spatiotemporal scaffold, allowing unitary perspective transformations while preserving global past/future in the studied setting.
This is direct evidence FOR the carrier-enlargement absorption hypothesis.
It materially lowers the plausibility of a generic H8R no-go.
But the result is scoped to pure process-matrix settings and does not establish the full finite-resource, open, non-Markovian, arbitrary-intervention theorem sought here.
13. FOUNDATION FAMILY K — 2026 HIGHER-ORDER EXTENSIONS
Apadula, Bisio, Chiribella, Perinotti, Simonov,
Higher-order transformations of bidirectional quantum processes,
arXiv:2602.00856 (2026).
This constructs a hierarchy of higher-order transformations for bistochastic / bidirectional processes, including settings with input-output direction and global causal-order indefiniteness.
Relevance:

It strengthens the methodological conclusion:

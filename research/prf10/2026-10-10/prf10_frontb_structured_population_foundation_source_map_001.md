0. FOUNDATION QUESTION
Broad field-level question, stripped of project notation:
WHEN CAN A CONTINUOUSLY STRUCTURED POPULATION OF INDIVIDUAL SOURCE CARRIERS, COUPLED THROUGH A COMMON DYNAMICAL ENVIRONMENT, BE REDUCED EXACTLY OR APPROXIMATELY TO A FINITE SET OF MACROSCOPIC VARIABLES WITHOUT LOSING A DECLARED TARGET?
Project binding will come only after the mature theory map.
The live Front B object motivating the question is:
a formation-time-tagged PBH population measure
dmu_B2(M_i,t_f)
whose members evaporate and source radiation/hadrons, while downstream hadronic and BBN variables share a common evolving state.
1. STABLE BACKBONE
A. STRUCTURED POPULATION THEORY
Metz & Diekmann (eds.), The Dynamics of Physiologically Structured Populations, LNBM 68, Springer, 1986.
Role:
monograph-level origin of physiologically structured population modeling.
Diekmann, Gyllenberg, Metz, Thieme,
On the formulation and analysis of general deterministic structured population models. I. Linear theory,
J. Math. Biol. 36 (1998) 349–388.
Diekmann, Gyllenberg, Huang, Kirkilionis, Metz, Thieme,
On the formulation and analysis of general deterministic structured population models. II. Nonlinear theory,
J. Math. Biol. 43 (2001) 157–189.
Core methodological idea of the nonlinear theory:
introduce an ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITION E(t) such that, when E(t) is prescribed, individuals are independent and the population equation is linear.
Nonlinearity is then generated by a feedback consistency relation in which population outputs determine the environment that in turn affects individual dynamics.
This is the first mature-theory match to the Front B problem.
B. FINITE-DIMENSIONAL REDUCTION
Diekmann, Gyllenberg, Metz,
Finite Dimensional State Representation of Linear and Nonlinear Delay Systems,
J. Dyn. Diff. Equat. 30 (2018) 1439–1467.
Question:
when can all relevant information in an infinite-dimensional delay/renewal system be obtained from finitely many ODE state variables?
Linear autonomous and linear-with-input cases:
necessary and sufficient conditions are developed.
Nonlinear cases:
sufficient conditions are developed.
Diekmann, Gyllenberg, Metz,
On models of physiologically structured populations and their reduction to ordinary differential equations,
J. Math. Biol. 80 (2020) 189–204.
Diekmann, Gyllenberg, Metz,
Finite dimensional state representation of physiologically structured populations,
J. Math. Biol. 80 (2020) 205–273.
These papers provide:
- exact ODE-reducibility as “without loss of relevant information”;
- output-relative state representation;
- explicit necessary/sufficient conditions under declared restrictions;
- catalogue of ODE-reducible one-dimensional deterministic i-state models.
C. POPULATION BALANCE THEORY
Ramkrishna,
Population Balances: Theory and Applications to Particulate Systems in Engineering,
Academic Press/Elsevier, 2000.
Classical population-balance language:
population density over internal coordinates;
transport in internal state;
birth/death/source/sink/aggregation/etc.;
macroscopic quantities obtained by integration against output kernels.
Modern numerical reviews confirm:
finite-moment methods are useful but GENERICALLY NOT EXACT because the moment hierarchy does not close.
Approximate closure, quadrature reconstruction and realizability become separate issues.
D. INVARIANT-OBSERVABLE / KOOPMAN PARALLEL
A general dynamical-systems parallel:
finite exact closure of selected observables corresponds to a finite-dimensional invariant subspace under the evolution/Koopman generator.
This is not needed as the primary Front B host, but it clarifies the structural principle:
EXACT FINITE REDUCTION
requires
CLOSURE OF THE DECLARED OBSERVABLE/OUTPUT SUBSPACE UNDER THE DYNAMICS.
Do not confuse this with generic data-driven Koopman approximation.
2. CANONICAL MATURE OBJECT
The mature structured-population object can be written schematically as:
population state:
mu_t on individual-state space Omega;
environment:
E(t) in finite- or infinite-dimensional environment space;
conditional population dynamics:
d mu_t / dt = L^*_{E(t)} mu_t
or its transport/renewal/PDE equivalent;
population outputs:
Y_j(t) = <mu_t, w_j(.,E(t))>;
environment feedback:
dE/dt = F(E,Y)
or a more general feedback consistency relation.
Key conceptual split:
WITH E(t) PRESCRIBED:
population dynamics may be linear in mu.
AFTER CLOSING E FROM POPULATION OUTPUTS:
full population+environment system is nonlinear.
This exactly explains why:
LINEAR SOURCE SUPERPOSITION
does not imply
LINEAR SOLUTION OR ENDPOINT SUPERPOSITION.
3. FINITE-DIMENSIONAL REDUCTION THEORY MAP
There are at least four distinct notions that must not be merged.
R0 — OUTPUT EVALUATION
At a fixed time, compute finitely many integrals <mu,w_j>.
No dynamic closure claim.
R1 — EXACT OUTPUT CLOSURE
The selected output vector z=P mu evolves by a closed finite-dimensional ODE for every legal environment history.
Operator form:
the span of output functionals must be invariant under the appropriate dual evolution/generator family, together with source/reproduction terms.
R2 — FULL RELEVANT-STATE REDUCTION
The finite state determines all population information declared scientifically relevant.
This is stronger than closing one source term.
R3 — APPROXIMATE CLOSURE
Finite moments/quadrature/surrogate variables approximate required outputs with an explicit error model.
This is not exact ODE-reducibility.
4. WHAT THE 2020 PSPM THEORY ACTUALLY SAYS
For deterministic one-dimensional individual state, the 2020 work derives a finite-dimensional representation by asking for weight functions w_i such that the output-function subspace is invariant under the adjoint transport/degradation operator for every environmental condition.
In one displayed specialization the condition has the form:
g(x,E) w_i'(x)
-
mu(x,E) w_i(x)
=
sum_j H_ij(E) w_j(x).
This is a concrete invariant-subspace closure condition.
Reproduction/source terms impose an additional invariant-subspace condition.
Under the paper's restrictions, the resulting catalogue is not merely sufficient but necessary.
Important lesson for IW:
EXACT FINITE REDUCTION IS STRUCTURALLY RESTRICTIVE.
It is not licensed just because only a few macroscopic quantities are ultimately wanted.
5. MOMENT-CLOSURE THEORY MAP
Population-balance literature distinguishes:
EXACT CLOSED MOMENT SYSTEM:
derivatives of retained moments depend only on retained moments.
UN CLOSED HIERARCHY:
retained moments depend on higher/unrepresented moments.
APPROXIMATE CLOSURE:
postulate/reconstruct missing information from retained moments.
QUADRATURE-BASED CLOSURE:
represent distribution by a finite atomic/quadrature object reproducing selected moments.
REALIZABILITY:
retained moments must correspond to an actual nonnegative distribution.
Therefore:
FINITE MOMENT SUMMARY
!=
EXACT POPULATION REDUCTION.
Moment closure is a method family whose scientific legality is target- and error-dependent.
6. PBH-SPECIFIC EASY CLASS FROM MATURE LITERATURE
The 2026 Carr–Iovino–Perna–Vaskonen–Veermäe review gives a clean contrast class for extended PBH mass functions.
For an observable A where each PBH contributes independently, the observable functional is linear in the mass distribution:
A[psi]
=
A_0
+
∫ d ln M A_1(M) psi(M).
In this class, monochromatic constraints can be transferred to an extended mass function by integrating against the monochromatic response kernel.
The review explicitly states the assumption:
EACH PBH CONTRIBUTES INDEPENDENTLY TO THE CONSTRAINED OBSERVABLE.
It also explicitly discusses limitations when collective/environment-sensitive effects violate this assumption.
This is the correct theorem-level meaning of the familiar extended-mass kernel rule.
Hence:
MONOCHROMATIC RESPONSE KERNEL INTEGRATION
is legal for a LINEAR-IN-DISTRIBUTION OBSERVABLE.
It is not a universal rule for any extended PBH endpoint.

12. EXACT SURVIVING RESEARCH QUESTION
After mature-host subtraction, the Front B theoretical residual can be stated sharply:
Let mu_t be the transported B2 PBH population measure and let
Q_1(mu_t),...,Q_m(mu_t)
be the finite family of PBH source functionals actually consumed by the admitted hadron/BBN state equations.
QUESTION A — EXACT CLOSURE
Does there exist a finite-dimensional family of population observables z(t) such that:
- all required Q_h are functions of z;
- z evolves autonomously given the declared shared environment/history;
- the closure remains valid for the legal B2 carrier class?
This is an invariant-output-subspace / ODE-reducibility question.
QUESTION B — TARGET-SPECIFIC APPROXIMATE CLOSURE
If exact closure fails, can a finite approximation z_N produce the light-element target with a certified error bound uniform over the legal B2 carrier class?
This is a model-reduction / moment-closure / approximation question.
QUESTION C — SOURCE BINDING
Does the actual source code/physics consume only population outputs covered by such a closure, or does state-dependent hadron feedback force more of the distribution history to remain explicit?
